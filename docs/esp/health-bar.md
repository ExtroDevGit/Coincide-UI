# Health Bar

Renders health next to or around targets.

## Main keys
- `HealthBar.Enabled`
- `HealthBar.Position` (`Left`, `Right`, `Top`, `Bottom`)
- `HealthBar.SideGap`
- `HealthBar.Width`
- `HealthBar.ShowText`
- `HealthBar.TextFollowBar`
- `HealthBar.HideWhenFullHP`
- `HealthBar.FollowGradientColorText`
- `HealthBar.Font`, `HealthBar.TextSize`
- `HealthBar.Outline.*`
- `HealthBar.Gradient.*`
